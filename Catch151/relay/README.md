# Buddy relay

Browsers can't send the Knomi's local Wi-Fi messages (UDP broadcasts on port 47115), so Catch 151 in a browser meets its buddies through this relay: a Cloudflare Worker with one Durable Object that passes the same 65-byte buddy messages (`buddy.h`) between devices on the same home network.

- **Grouping:** devices are grouped by public address: their IPv4, or their IPv6 /64 plus the IPv4 they report (computers and phones often connect over IPv6, Knomis over IPv4).
- **What it passes:** only well-formed buddy messages, at most 8 a second per device, 16 devices per group. It logs nothing, and every check on the messages stays on the devices.
- **What it keeps:** the leaderboard (`src/board.js`): each minigame's best ten, with the gamer tag each came with and the day, and the heaviest fish anyone has landed in Reel Fishing (its weight, its kind and if it was shiny), and the record for each kind of fish (since 2.2.11). One entry a device, kept by a hash of its id; nothing about who or where. Each device sends its own bests (`BM_SCORE` messages, and `BM_FISH` for its heaviest fish and, since 2.2.11, its heaviest of each kind, a few a second, to the relay alone and never passed on) when it connects, at each game's end, and when its gamer tag changes. A score past what its game can reach is ignored, as is a weight its kind can't reach (over 1.2 times its Pokédex weight) or a Pokémon that isn't a Beach fish.
- **The board on the devices (2.2.15):** a device's Games menu has a page for each game's best three. It asks when the menu opens (a `BM_BOARD`, type 19, to the relay alone: one game, or `0xFF` for all) and the relay answers each game in a `BM_BOARD` of its own, to that device alone (`boardReply()` in `src/board.js`): the three scores, the tags as the board shows them (`****` for a rude one; none without a tag), each one's month and day, and Reel Fishing's first fish. Copies older than this drop it, and a relay older than this passes the ask on to the devices in its home, which drop it too.
- **The first of a game:** when a device's best is the first of its game's ten, the relay answers on its connection with a `BM_SCORE` of its own (from `0xFFFFFFFF`, to that device): the game gives it the High Score medal, the golden cartridge, once. Copies older than the leaderboard drop it.
- **Started over (2.2.10):** only scores and fish marked as made since then (the message's `turn` byte = `BOARD_SEASON`, `src/buddy.h`; `SEASON` in `src/board.js`) count, kept under `scores1`, `fishTop1` and `fishKinds1` (each kind's record: it started from `fishTop1`'s fish). The board before it (from 2.2.9's all-time records, some from games still changing) is still in storage as `scores`, `fishTop` and `fish`, and nothing reads it. The heaviest fish in `/stats` comes from the new board.
- **No tag, no place (2.2.19):** a score or fish sent without a gamer tag isn't taken, and the game stops sending them until a tag is picked (it asks after each scored game until then). Entries kept from before without a tag are hidden everywhere (`/stats`, `/board.svg`, the devices' pages), not deleted: one shows again with its tag once its device picks one, as a device sends all its bests when its tag changes. Each game's ten are ten with tags.
- **Gamer tags:** up to four characters. A tag on the rude-word list (the same as the game's, `src/gamertag.h`, read through stand-ins like 5 for S) shows as `****`; the list here can grow without an update to the game. `node --test relay/test/*.test.mjs` checks the board's rules, and that both lists match.
- **Cost:** it tells each device how many others share its group (`{"n": 2}`), and a device that is alone sends nothing. A `ping` text keeps quiet connections open and is answered without waking the object. Cloudflare's free plan covers it; if it ever runs out, only relay buddies pause until the next day, and the games keep working.

- **Live count:** [`/stats`](https://catch151-relay.fedor-dd0.workers.dev/stats) says how many devices are connected right now, all kinds together: `{"online": 12}`. That's every Knomi on Wi-Fi, Mac and Windows app and open browser tab, so it counts players who are playing now, not everyone who has the game. Only connections heard from in the last two minutes count (each device pings every 45 s), so one whose Wi-Fi dropped without closing isn't counted. It's only the number, refreshed at most once a minute, and it's the one count: the leaderboard page and the README's badge show it, and the board's picture has none.
- **Biggest fish:** `/stats` also has the heaviest fish, in pounds as the game shows it: `{"online": 12, "fish": {"lb": 308.6, "kind": "Cloyster", "shiny": false, "day": "2026-11-16"}, "biggest": "308.6 lb Cloyster"}` (`"biggest": "none yet"` until the first). (The README showed `biggest` on a badge until 2.2.10; the minigame leaderboard has it now.)

- **Leaderboard:** `/stats` has it too, as `"board": {"games": [{"game": 6, "name": "Pinball", "top": [{"tag": "FED", "score": 48250, "shown": "48,250", "day": "2026-11-16"}, ...]}, ...], "fish": [{"tag": "ZOE", "lb": 551.2, "kind": "Gyarados", "shiny": false, "day": "2026-11-16"}, ...], "kinds": [{"dex": 54, "kind": "Psyduck", "lb": 46.3, "shiny": true, "tag": "ZOE", "day": "2026-11-18"}, {"dex": 55, "kind": "Golduck", "lb": null}, ...]}` (`kinds`: every kind, in Pokédex order; `lb` null for one nobody has landed yet), and as a picture, [`/board.svg`](https://catch151-relay.fedor-dd0.workers.dev/board.svg): each game's best in the game's own font with its cartridge (`src/board_art.js`, made by `tools/relay_board_art.sh`), kept five minutes. The README shows the picture, and the website's [scores page](https://fedorsosnin.github.io/Catch151/leaderboard/) the top tens and each fish's record.

The fish and the board need the `Lobby` class's code, which runs in `pokeknomigochi-relay`: deploy both (`wrangler deploy`, then `wrangler deploy -c wrangler-catch151.toml`) before a release whose copies send them.

Deploy with [Wrangler](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/wrangler/): `cd relay && wrangler deploy`.

**Two addresses, one lobby (since 2.2.0, the rename to Catch 151):** `pokeknomigochi-relay` (`wrangler.toml`) holds the
lobby, and the copies up to 2.1.4 connect to it. `catch151-relay` (`wrangler-catch151.toml`, deploy with
`wrangler deploy -c wrangler-catch151.toml`) runs the same code (`src/catch151.js`) and reaches the same lobby through
its `LOBBY` binding's `script_name`, so copies on either address meet. Never rename `wrangler.toml`'s Worker: that
would start a second, empty lobby.
